Footprint

Building the structural backbone of India's railways, power infrastructure & industrial corridors since 2016.

M/s Nageshwar Jaiswal is a Nagpur-based sole proprietorship led by Shri Nageshwar Budhi Jaiswal. Since 2016, the firm has established itself as a premier EPC contractor executing major packages for India's foremost Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) and leading private industrial corporations — delivering mission-critical railway infrastructure, power plant civil packages, and heavy engineering projects across Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, and Uttar Pradesh.

Our engagements span railway P-Way & siding development, 61-metre open web girder fabrication & erection, FGD civil packages for 2×250 MW power stations, mega earthwork formations, and bituminous road networks — all substantiated by official LOAs, work orders, and completion certificates.

Core Expertise

What We Build

Five integrated EPC domains — from precision railway track renewal and 61-metre open web girder erection to mega earthworks and FGD civil packages for 2×250 MW power plants.

01
Railway Infrastructure & P-Way Engineering
Complete Track Renewal (CTR), railway siding development, mechanized ballast supply & stacking, EUR 52-rake maintenance facilities, wagon tippler rail lines, and ballastless track at industrial silo yards.
Complete Track Renewal Railway Sidings Ballast Supply & Stacking EUR Rake Facility Wagon Tippler
02
Bridges, Girders & Road Under Bridges
Fabrication, assembly & launching of 61-metre Open Web Girders; Road Under Bridges (RUB) by hydraulic box pushing; PSC slab bridges (6.1m span); Foot Over Bridges (FOB); minor & major bridges.
61m Open Web Girder RUB Box Pushing PSC Slab Bridges FOB Erection RCC Box Culverts
03
Heavy Industrial & Power Plant Civil Packages
Flue Gas Desulfurization (FGD) civil systems for 2×250 MW thermal stations; LHP & GHP civil packages; heavy machine foundations for vibration & dynamic loads; S&T control buildings; loco maintenance sheds; RMC supply.
FGD Civil Package Heavy Foundations S&T Buildings Loco Maintenance Sheds
04
Mega Earthworks, Drainage & Formation
3.50+ Lakh m³ bulk earthwork excavation & compacted embankment; RRM & precast concrete U-drains; box culverts; Hume pipe culverts; retaining walls; stone pitching; railway formation & cutting.
3.5L m³ Earthwork Precast U-Drains Box Culverts Railway Formation
05
Roads, Industrial Pavements & Site Development
Bituminous Concrete (BC) roads; DLC & PQC rigid pavements; heavy-duty interlocking paver blocks for container yards; GSB, WMM, DBM, Seal Coat; compound walls; station beautification & passenger amenities.
Bituminous Roads PQC / DLC Pavements Industrial Paving Station Works
